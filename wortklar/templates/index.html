    Willkommen bei Wortklar!

    Wortklar is your companion for learning German. Look up any word or inflected form to see clear English meanings, crucial A1 grammar (articles, plurals, auxiliary verbs), and authentic bilingual examples.

    Quick Tips for Learners

    • Type 4+ letters: Autocomplete suggestions appear as a keyboard list without replacing your input.
    • Short words work directly: Press Enter or click Search to look up short words like in, Ei, or bin.
    • Inflected forms: Search forms directly (e.g. Häuser → Haus, ging → gehen, besser → gut).
    • Spelling assistance: Typos and missing umlauts (hauser, shcön, schon vs schön) are clearly explained.

    Wortklar uses real lexicographical data from English Wiktionary and German Wiktionary, extracted via Kaikki.org / Wiktextract.
